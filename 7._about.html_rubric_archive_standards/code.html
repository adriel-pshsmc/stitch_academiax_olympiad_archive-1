Tri-Pillar Evaluation Matrix

Senate Inscription Scoring Rubric

Every candidate text receives independent scoring from 1 to 5 stars across three orthogonal dimensions. Submissions failing to achieve at least 3.0 aggregate in any single column are remanded for secondary author revision.

I

Mathematical & Scientific Accuracy

Weight: 40% Total Inscription
Score Tier Classification Evaluation Thresholds & Lemma Rigor
1 Star (★) Unverified / Flawed Fatal logical discontinuities, ungrounded inductive steps, unchecked boundary conditions, or unverified theorem invocations. Total rejection.
2 Stars (★★) Preliminary Soundness General mathematical argument holds, but peripheral proofs omit critical counterexample exclusions or neglect edge-domain definitions.
3 Stars (★★★) Rigorous Baseline All propositions verified with complete derivations. Symbolic notation adheres throughout to standard Bourbaki or ISO 80000-2 mathematical conventions.
4 Stars (★★★★) Exemplary Proofwork Includes exhaustive lemma demonstrations, explicit boundary validations, multi-path proof validations, and zero unresolved analytical tensions.
5 Stars (★★★★★) Imperial Laureate Level Demonstrates flawless formal consistency, novel problem-reduction techniques, fully generalized corollary extensions, and publication-ready proofs.
II

Pedagogical Depth & Articulation

Weight: 35% Total Inscription
Score Tier Classification Educational Clarity & PSHS Syllabus Accord
1 Star (★) Obtuse / Opaque Dense prose without modular scaffolding. Inaccessible to students; lacks intermediate intuition steps or contextual motivation.
2 Stars (★★) Formulaic Rote Lists formulas mechanically without conceptual exposition. Omits geometric interpretations, physics analogies, or concrete problem models.
3 Stars (★★★) Structured Syllabus Fit Direct alignment with PSHS Main Campus curricula (Grades 11-12 Advanced Stem / Olympiad tracks). Clear topic sequencing and guided problem sets.
4 Stars (★★★★) Heuristic Masterclass Provides intuitive scaffolding, anticipated misconceptions, step-by-step diagnostic breakdown, and graded difficulty progression.
5 Stars (★★★★★) Definitive Canon Reference Exceptional intellectual clarity. Elevates complex abstract notions into accessible mental frameworks; serves as campus-wide study syllabus benchmark.
III

LaTeX & Typographic Standards

Weight: 25% Total Inscription
Score Tier Classification INRIA Compliance, TikZ Quality & BibTeX Integrity
1 Star (★) Non-Conforming Low-resolution raster screenshots of equations, word processor artifacts, unreferenced citations, or corrupt TeX source compilation.
2 Stars (★★) Raw TeX Compile Compiles cleanly under pdfLaTeX, but violates margin gutters, uses non-standard line spreads, or features low-contrast vector figures.
3 Stars (★★★) HAL Node Standard Strict compliance with INRIA preprint template. Vector figures (pgf/TikZ or clean SVG), properly mapped BibTeX DOIs, and zero typographical collisions.
4 Stars (★★★★) Imprimerie Precision Flawless microtypography (kerning, hyphenation penalties, hanging punctuation via microtype). Sharp vector charts with uniform monochromatic palette.
5 Stars (★★★★★) Imperial Cartulary Edition Bespoke algorithmic diagramming, PDF/A-1b metadata embedded, fully reproducible TeX tree, and immaculate mathematical typesetting elegance.
